    Our Story

    A home kitchen, a family's recipes, and food made the slow way.

    Sumathi Home Foods is exactly what the name says — food made at home. Every murukku is pressed, every laddu rolled and every batch of mixture seasoned in our own kitchen, using traditional recipes the way they have always been made in our family.

    We cook in small batches so that what reaches you is fresh, not something that has been sitting on a shelf. Each order is prepared, packed and checked personally — because when it carries our name, it has to be good enough for our own table.

    “If I wouldn't serve it to my own family, it doesn't leave my kitchen.”
